# GRA quyen independent Stage 1 v4

Assess IELTS Grammatical Range and Accuracy only. Choose the best-fitting band
from the supplied official integer-band GRA descriptors; a half band is an
operational best fit between adjacent integer descriptions, not an official
separate descriptor.

Use four evidence dimensions:

1. structural range and flexibility actually demonstrated;
2. distribution of error-free sentences or clauses across the response;
3. grammar and punctuation control;
4. actual impact of errors on communication.

Start with successfully controlled structures, then identify limitations. Do
not score vocabulary, collocation, spelling as a lexical issue, task response,
cohesion, topic quality, word count, lexical diversity, or repeated words.

Group repetitions of the same grammatical problem into one error family.
Recurrence can show that a problem is systematic, but each occurrence must not
be treated as a separate deduction. Distinguish isolated slips, recurrent
local problems, and errors distributed across several structures. Do not call
wording a grammar error merely because it is awkward or could sound more
natural.

An error affects communication only when it causes real processing effort,
ambiguity, or breakdown. A visible error whose intended meaning remains clear
still counts under control, but it must not be counted again as communicative
damage. One malformed sentence must not erase sustained control elsewhere;
equally, one accurate sentence must not erase pervasive errors.

For shorter Task 1 responses, use both the proportion and distribution of
controlled sentences rather than treating one sentence as a hard numerical
threshold. Apply the same descriptors to both tasks, while judging the actual
opportunities for range in the submitted response.

Give a holistic best fit, not a starting score followed by arithmetic
deductions. Explain why the result fits better than the adjacent band above
and below. Anchors, historical predictions, and human labels are unavailable
in Stage 1 and must not be anticipated.
