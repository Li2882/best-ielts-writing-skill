雅思作文题目：2011年10月考试真题

In many countries, the proportion of older people is steadily increasing. Does this trend have more positive or negative effects on society?

考生原文

Nowadays, an increasing number of people are concerned about the aging population. However, whether the population aging makes a positive influence or a negative impact has caused heated debate. As to this topic, I mainly agree with the latter view for the following reasons.

First, it is obvious that the increasing share of aging population has become an emergency problem for sustainable economy development. As we all know, workforce is an essential part of industries, such as the house construction and manufacture. This means that government will be confronted with extra heavy economic burden brought by labour shortage. Therefore, it is necessary to find a decent solution to cope with this upset situation.

Moreover, for the old generation is getting aged, the young will have heavy stress of life. Take the situation in china for example, each young couple has to make enough money to afford four old persons and one child’s lives, such as the fees for expensive healthy care of the old, the tuition for the child’s education and even the daily living costs. Beside that, the serious competition in the work will add the pressure to youth.

This is not to say that an aging population is all useless. Of course, they will be helpful to make society in the rapid development, when enormous young people pour into careers with the advanced knowledge. But the elderly also have rich experience, which is an indispensable part of the work.

In conclusion, the population aging will impose heavy economic burden on the young and cause serious labour shortage. Meanwhile, we should not ignore the important roles which the aging population is undertaking. And it is integrant for government to raise a policy to reach the balance between the old and the young.

批改 by Angela令狐

本次批改严格按照IELTS小作文评分标准进行。IELTS小作文评分项：TA (内容的完整性), CC (连贯性及一致性), GRA (语法范围及精准度), LR (词汇资源)。 文末会进行总评及打分。

总评分5.5分（TA5.0 CC5.5 LR5.5 GRA6.0）

1. 内容没有偏题现象，但是有一个重大理解失误：aging population绝对不等于老年人！！！Aging指的是人口老龄这一个现象，涉及到整个社会阶层，而不仅仅是老人。（例平均退休年龄变大） 2. 语法基本没有问题，有复杂句式的使用以及变化。 3. 用词简单但是准确，但重复率略高。建议积累各个话题词汇；偶尔一些不正式以及过度概括的表达出现，以后要避免。 4. 分段方法准确，连词使用良好。但是内容之间偶尔出现逻辑不紧密的现象（如第二段中指出的）。建议在使用连词的同时思考内容逻辑联系。
