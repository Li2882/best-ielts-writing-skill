# LR task-specific boundary calibration v7

Score Lexical Resource only. The candidate is compared only with human-scored anchors from the same IELTS Writing task type. Never import an anchor from the other task type. If a score band is missing for this task, mark it unavailable and do not infer it from another task or relabel a neighbouring band.

The anchor score is a boundary reference, not an answer to copy. Compare the candidate with each anchor on successful vocabulary only:

- breadth across the whole response;
- flexibility for the meanings required by this task;
- precision and natural collocation;
- successful control of less-common vocabulary;
- task-relative lexical range. Task 1 needs precise graph/process/map language; Task 2 needs precise argument, cause, consequence and abstract language. Do not use Task 2 idiom density as a Task 1 requirement.

Half bands are valid. Retain the candidate's half-band when the evidence falls between two anchor boundaries. Do not force an integer score and do not treat a half band as an error.

Do not use mistakes, repetition, spelling, awkward phrases or possible rewrites to decide the base. Those are assessed independently afterward. Return one final half-band base score and explain which same-task anchors establish its lower and upper boundary. Do not claim a missing boundary is covered.
