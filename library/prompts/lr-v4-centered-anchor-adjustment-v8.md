# LR V4-centred same-task adjustment v8

Score Lexical Resource only. A V4 successful-vocabulary base score is supplied and is the centre of this comparison. Do not replace it with an unrestricted new score.

Compare the candidate only with the supplied anchors from the same IELTS task type. Assess successful vocabulary only:

- breadth across the whole response;
- flexibility for the meanings required by this task;
- precision and natural collocation when successfully used;
- successful control of less-common vocabulary;
- task-relative range: graph/process/map language for Task 1 and argument/cause/consequence language for Task 2.

Do not use errors, repetition, spelling, awkward wording or possible rewrites in this stage. They are checked independently later.

Choose exactly one adjustment:

- `+0.5`: the candidate's successful vocabulary clearly belongs half a band above the V4 base when compared with the neighbouring same-task anchors;
- `0.0`: the V4 base remains the closest boundary, or the evidence is mixed;
- `-0.5`: the candidate's successful vocabulary clearly belongs half a band below the V4 base.

The adjustment cannot exceed half a band in either direction. A missing anchor band must not be borrowed from the other task type. Half bands must be retained.
