# Scoring Rules

## Shared calibration

- Score each criterion independently from 0 to 9 in 0.5 increments.
- Select the descriptor matching the overall performance. Do not default to the lower band when two bands seem plausible.
- Occasional local errors can coexist with Bands 7–8. Reserve Band 6 language scores for merely adequate range or repeated control problems across the response.
- Measure error frequency as a proportion of sentences: most sentences means frequent; a minority means occasional; a handful across the response means rare.
- Keep criterion boundaries separate. A task-coverage flaw must not automatically reduce vocabulary or grammar.
- Calculate the overall estimate as the arithmetic mean of assessable criteria, rounded to the nearest 0.5; exact .25 and .75 midpoints round upward.

## Academic Task 1

### Task Achievement

- Band 5: mechanical data recount, no clear overview, or important inaccuracies.
- Band 6: addresses the task, but the overview is unclear or key features are insufficiently covered.
- Band 7: clear overview; main trends or differences are selected and supported by data.
- Band 8: all key features are skilfully selected and clearly illustrated.
- Band 9: all requirements are fully satisfied with a fully developed overview.

### Coherence and Cohesion

- Band 5: faulty or mechanical linking and weak organisation.
- Band 6: coherent overall, with some faulty or mechanical cohesion.
- Band 7: logical organisation, clear progression, and appropriate linking.
- Band 8: skilfully managed sequencing and cohesion.
- Band 9: seamless cohesion that attracts no attention.

### Lexical Resource

- Band 5: limited range with noticeable errors.
- Band 6: adequate range with some inaccurate word choice.
- Band 7: flexible and reasonably precise data language, with occasional inaccuracies.
- Band 8: wide, natural, precise resource with rare slips.
- Band 9: full flexibility and precision with only rare minor slips.

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- Band 5: limited structures and frequent errors.
- Band 6: simple and complex forms are mixed; errors rarely block meaning.
- Band 7: varied structures and frequent error-free sentences.
- Band 8: wide range with most sentences error-free.
- Band 9: full flexibility and accuracy with only rare minor slips.

### Task 1 checks

- Under 150 words: Task Achievement cannot exceed 5.0.
- A missing or unclear overview commonly limits Task Achievement to about Band 6.
- Describing another chart or inventing data caps Task Achievement at 5.0.
- A format or paragraphing flaw affects its relevant criteria, not all four.
- Without the chart or complete data description, leave Task Achievement unassessed.

## Academic Task 2

### Task Response

- Band 5: position is unclear, part of the task is missed, or ideas remain underdeveloped.
- Band 6: position is relevant, but conclusions may be unclear or main ideas insufficiently developed.
- Band 7: clear position throughout, with main ideas extended and supported.
- Band 8: well-developed response with relevant, extended, supported ideas.
- Band 9: fully developed position with relevant, fully extended, well-supported ideas.

### Coherence and Cohesion

- Band 5: faulty or mechanical linking and weak paragraphing.
- Band 6: coherent overall, but cohesion or referencing may be faulty or mechanical.
- Band 7: logical progression, appropriate linking, and a clear central topic in each paragraph.
- Band 8: skilful sequencing and well-managed paragraphing.
- Band 9: seamless cohesion that attracts no attention.

### Lexical Resource

- Band 5: limited range and noticeable errors that may cause difficulty.
- Band 6: adequate range with some inaccurate word choice or collocation.
- Band 7: flexibility and precision, including some less common language, with occasional inaccuracies.
- Band 8: wide, natural, precise resource with rare slips.
- Band 9: full flexibility and natural, sophisticated control with only rare minor slips.

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

- Band 5: limited range with frequent errors.
- Band 6: simple and complex forms are mixed; errors rarely block meaning.
- Band 7: varied complex structures, frequent error-free sentences, and good control.
- Band 8: wide range with most sentences error-free and only occasional slips.
- Band 9: full flexibility and accuracy with only rare minor slips.

### Task 2 checks

- A reason becomes developed when supported by a concrete example, consequence, or causal explanation. Do not call it underdeveloped merely because it is concise.
- Mechanical sequencers can limit Coherence and Cohesion, but clear progression and effective paragraphing should not fall below Band 7 for that flaw alone.
- A few collocation, spelling, or verb-pattern errors do not justify Band 6 when range is wide and control is otherwise strong.
- Under 250 words: Task Response cannot exceed 6.0. Under 150 words: it cannot exceed 5.0.
- A response that largely ignores the question, answers another question, or relies on a memorised template caps Task Response at 5.0.
- Without the prompt, Task Response is unassessed. Calculate the overall estimate from the other three criteria only and do not emit Task Response blockers.
- Treat a criterion spread greater than 3 bands as a validation failure requiring reassessment.
