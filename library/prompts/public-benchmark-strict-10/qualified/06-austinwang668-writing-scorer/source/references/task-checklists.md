## Academic Task 1

### Required evidence

Require the complete instructions and readable chart, table, map, plan, process, or diagram. Without the source visual, do not score Task Achievement or the task overall.

### Inspect before rating

- Identify the visual type, time frame, units, categories, and requested comparison.
- Establish the actual key features: major trends, extremes, meaningful comparisons, important changes, or main stages.
- Check whether the response provides an overview rather than only item-by-item detail.
- Verify every reported figure, direction, stage, and comparison against the source.
- Check selection and grouping: reward relevant summarisation, not exhaustive transcription.
- Check connected prose and an academic or neutral register; do not require a conclusion that the task does not need.

### Task Achievement anchors

- **9:** Satisfy every requirement fully and appropriately, with only extremely rare content lapses.
- **8:** Select and present key features skilfully, accurately, and sufficiently; tolerate only occasional omissions.
- **7:** Cover the requirements with a clear overview, sound categorisation, and highlighted main trends or differences; allow minor omissions or limited illustration.
- **6:** Focus on the task, attempt a relevant overview, and cover selected key features with supporting data, though selection or detail may be uneven.
- **5:** Address the task generally but cover key features inadequately, recount details mechanically, or omit a meaningful overview or data support.
- **4:** Make an identifiable attempt but select few key features; include important irrelevant, repetitive, or inaccurate material.
- **3:** Misunderstand major parts of the visual or task, present little relevant information, or repeat limited content.
- **2:** Produce content that barely relates to the visual or requested report.
- **1:** Write 20 words or fewer or produce content wholly unrelated to the task.
- **0:** Make no attempt, write wholly in another language, or provide proven total memorisation.

Treat a missing clear overview as a major boundary issue: the public IELTS preparation material identifies a clear overview as important for Band 7 or above. Do not treat an introduction that merely paraphrases the prompt as an overview.

## General Training Task 1

### Required evidence

Require the full situation, intended recipient, purpose, every bullet point, and candidate letter. Without them, do not score Task Achievement or the task overall.

### Inspect before rating

- Identify the relationship with the recipient and the required personal, semi-formal, or formal tone.
- Break every bullet point into all requested functions; plural requests require adequate plural coverage.
- Check whether the purpose is immediately clear and remains consistent.
- Check whether each bullet point is covered, extended, and illustrated with relevant detail.
- Check letter conventions, greeting, sign-off, and connected prose without imposing one universal format where several are acceptable.
- Judge tone for appropriacy and consistency, not for formality alone.

### Task Achievement anchors

- **9:** Satisfy all requirements fully and appropriately, with only extremely rare content lapses.
- **8:** Present and extend every bullet point clearly and appropriately, with a clear purpose and well-controlled tone.
- **7:** Cover and highlight every bullet point, maintain a clear purpose and consistent appropriate tone, but allow minor limits in extension.
- **6:** Cover every bullet point adequately and make the purpose generally clear; allow missing detail or minor tone inconsistency.
- **5:** Present all bullet points but develop one or more inadequately; let purpose or tone become unclear or variable.
- **4:** Omit at least one bullet point, confuse the purpose, use unsuitable tone or format, or rely on irrelevant or repetitive content.
- **3:** Fail to address the situation meaningfully and present little relevant functional content.
- **2:** Produce a message that barely relates to the situation.
- **1:** Write 20 words or fewer or produce a wholly unrelated response.
- **0:** Make no attempt, write wholly in another language, or provide proven total memorisation.

## Task 2

Use the same Task Response anchors for Academic and General Training. Judge the appropriacy of style in context; General Training may permit a somewhat more personal style, but still requires a developed essay response.

### Required evidence

Require the complete topic statement and every instruction or question. Without the complete prompt, do not score Task Response or the task overall.

### Inspect before rating

- Decompose the prompt into every main part before reading the response.
- Identify the required operation: opinion, discussion, advantages/disadvantages, problems/solutions, two questions, or a combination.
- Track the writer's position from introduction through conclusion.
- Test each main idea for relevance, extension, and support rather than rewarding the number of ideas.
- Check whether examples actually support the claim and remain relevant to the prompt.
- Check essay format and connected prose; do not demand one memorised paragraph template.

### Task Response anchors

- **9:** Address and explore the prompt in depth, present a fully developed direct position, and extend and support relevant ideas comprehensively.
- **8:** Address the prompt appropriately and sufficiently with a clear, well-developed position and well-supported relevant ideas; allow occasional small lapses.
- **7:** Address the main parts, maintain a clear developed position, and extend and support relevant ideas, though precision or focus may lapse.
- **6:** Address the main parts, present a directly relevant position, and offer relevant ideas, but leave some parts, conclusions, or support unclear or underdeveloped.
- **5:** Address the prompt incompletely, present a position whose development is unclear, and offer limited or insufficiently developed ideas with some irrelevant detail or repetition.
- **4:** Tackle the prompt minimally or tangentially; make the position hard to locate and the main ideas difficult to identify, support, or connect to the task.
- **3:** Fail to address the prompt adequately, offer no clear position, and present only a few largely irrelevant or undeveloped ideas.
- **2:** Barely respond, offer no discernible position, and provide at most one or two undeveloped ideas.
- **1:** Write 20 words or fewer or produce a wholly unrelated response.
- **0:** Make no attempt, write wholly in another language, or provide proven total memorisation.

Missing a main part is a strong limiter. The official preparation material states that a response that fails to discuss all main parts can be limited below Band 6 even when its language is stronger.

## Full Writing estimate

- Require both complete tasks from the same test sitting or practice set.
- Score each task independently before weighting.
- Use Task 1 as one third and Task 2 as two thirds of the estimate.
- Report each task band and the combined result so the weighting remains auditable.
- Do not combine an Academic Task 1 response with a General Training Task 2 response unless the user explicitly requests a hypothetical calculation; label such a result hypothetical.
